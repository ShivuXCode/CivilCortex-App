# CivilCortex Field Privacy & Safety SOP v0.1

## 1. Civil Engineering Safety Protocol
CivilCortex is an inspection-assistance system, NOT a substitute for professional site-safety procedures.

**MANDATORY RULES:**
- **No Unsafe Access:** Do not instruct personnel to enter dangerous, confined, or unstable areas.
- **No Contact:** Do not physically touch or interact with potentially unstable structural elements.
- **No Climbing:** No elevated captures without proper fall-arrest equipment and authorization.
- **No Destructive Testing:** Do not chip, hammer, or scrape surfaces to expose defects.

## 2. Privacy & PII
Field photography in operational buildings frequently captures accidental Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

**MANDATORY RULES:**
- Avoid capturing faces, license plates, personal documents, or addresses during image acquisition.
- If PII is captured, it MUST be rejected or irreversibly redacted via pixelation/blurring BEFORE entering the ML dataset.
- Do not store unnecessary personal information (e.g., inspector home addresses, resident names) in metadata.
- Explicit consent must be obtained and documented for all participating field sites.
